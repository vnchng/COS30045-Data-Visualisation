The Price of Socioeconomic Health Inequality

An exploration of healthcare expenditure, healthcare coverage and socioeconomic differences in health outcomes across OECD countries.

Research question: Does greater healthcare expenditure correspond with better healthcare coverage and more equitable health outcomes?

01 / HEALTHCARE EXPENDITURE

Where does healthcare spending go?

Compare healthcare expenditure across OECD countries and examine how spending is distributed between different healthcare providers.

Healthcare expenditure by provider

Healthcare expenditure as a percentage of GDP, grouped by healthcare provider and country.

How to read: Each bar represents healthcare expenditure as a percentage of GDP. Colours show how expenditure is distributed between healthcare providers. Hover over a country to examine its expenditure composition.

02 / HEALTHCARE COVERAGE

Does greater spending correspond with better coverage?

Compare healthcare expenditure and healthcare coverage across countries to examine whether greater spending corresponds with broader access to healthcare.

Healthcare expenditure and coverage

Comparing healthcare expenditure with healthcare coverage across OECD countries.

How to read: Each point represents a particular country. Users can spotlight specific countries by manually selecting datapoints from the dropdown. Compare countries with similar levels of healthcare expenditure to examine differences in healthcare coverage.

How does perceived health differ by education level?

Compare perceived health outcomes across different levels of educational attainment to explore socioeconomic differences in health.

Perceived health status by education level

How to read: Each cell represents perceived health status for a country and education level. Compare the colour intensity between education groups to identify socioeconomic differences in perceived health.

DATA & METHODOLOGY

Data and methodology

This project uses OECD healthcare and socioeconomic data to explore relationships between healthcare expenditure, healthcare coverage and inequalities in perceived health.

Healthcare expenditure

Visualisation 1 uses OECD healthcare expenditure data to compare expenditure across countries and examine how spending is distributed between different healthcare providers.

Expenditure and coverage

Visualisation 2 combines two datasets: healthcare expenditure and healthcare coverage. The datasets were cleaned and joined by country and year, allowing expenditure and coverage to be compared within the same visualisation.

Socioeconomic health inequality

Visualisation 3 uses OECD perceived health data grouped by educational attainment. Education level is used as a socioeconomic measure to compare differences in perceived health across countries.

Data preparation

The datasets were cleaned before visualisation by selecting relevant variables, standardising values and filtering the data to the categories required for each visualisation. A combined dataset was also created for Visualisation 2 by matching expenditure and coverage observations by country and year.

Data sources

Data for this project was sourced from the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD).
